## Goal

Continuously generate behaviorally distinct single-mechanism Mickey arms, screen and independently confirm them against immutable matched controls on CPU, then make only production-clean and rollback-ready candidates eligible for local Mac promotion.

## Primary Metric

Confirmed matched-cell advantage rate, with zero K1Z harm, rejects, unexplained holds, or identity drift.
