# odin ledger

This is the public chronicle of the campaign. It records only events that changed
the agent, the field, or the standard of proof. Strategy detail belongs in the
experiment ledger; unsupported legend does not enter here.

## the held line

`oli-codex-proxywar:v14` became the champion baseline and survived every later
challenger through v27. When Softmax memberships were found to stack instead of
replace, thirteen old seats were retired. One active policy remained. The score
board stopped confusing an archive with an army.

## s1gtyr-k3rn

v27 crossed into the canonical Frontier executor used by Keystone and enabled
one economy bootstrap. In the local weak-seat trial it built City at turn 400,
Factory at 900, and won four seeded runs. The same-seat default control lost with
699 tiles.

The hosted field broke the omen. Against James, Auri v5, and Richard, v27 went
1/4. It still opened the economy on time, but transient social and pressure
actions expired before execution, producing 114 fallbacks and 111 holds. The
local victory was kept as evidence; the hosted verdict denied promotion.

## v4rdr-v4kt

v28 begins at the wire boundary. Overlapping platform requests are coalesced so
a superseded decision cannot arrive after its legal action has vanished. The
economy engine remains unchanged. Threshold tuning waits until action delivery
is clean.

The wire grew quieter: fallbacks fell from 114 to 25 and holds from 111 to 30.
The field did not yield. v28 went 0/4. The repair remains; the banner does not.

## skuld-v4kn

v29 invokes the earlier reckoning identified in the Frontier source review. A
bordering rival at 1.3 times our share can arm the behind-and-falling strike, and
escape banking ends after one cycle. It must turn a shrinking seat into pressure
without feeding the leader.

## the reviewer lane

On Oli's word a second line opened beside the build lane: Claude reads source and
replays, owns no seat, and may not touch league state. Its first pass verified the
v14 baseline and all six candidate audits at e2b5f65, then killed an arm class
before it burned episodes: pods run the executor in-process, but the server builds
the legal-action menu — menu-side tunables are dead letters in a pod env.

When the hosted field broke v27, the reviewer lane traced repeated stale social
and pressure IDs into HOLD substitutions. v28's request coalescing reduced the
damage sharply but did not erase it, and the policy went 0/4. The transport repair
stays. With action delivery improved and measured, the source-reviewed
behind-and-falling pair becomes the next isolated arm.

## standing law

Whenever a result changes policy direction, exposes a mechanism, establishes a
rival shift, earns a promotion, or kills a candidate, update this chronicle and
the merit ledger in the same checkpoint commit.
